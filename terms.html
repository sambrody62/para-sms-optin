Para sends account notification text messages: task reminders, daily task digests, calendar event reminders, task confirmations, and replies to requests the subscriber sends it. Opting out

Reply STOP (or END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT) to any message to cancel at any time. After you reply STOP, you will receive one final message confirming you are unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent. Reply START to resubscribe.
