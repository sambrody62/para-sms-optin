Program description

Para — Personal Task Assistant, operated by Sam Brody, sends account notification text messages (SMS): task reminders, daily task digests, calendar event reminders, and task confirmations. Messages are sent only to phone numbers registered through the opt-in form at this website. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your tasks and calendar — typically a few messages per day, up to 10 messages per day.

Fees

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan. Para itself does not charge for messages. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel at any time. After you reply STOP, you will receive a final message confirming your unsubscription, and no further messages will be sent.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or email sambrody34@gmail.com for assistance.

Carriers

Supported carriers include all major US carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
