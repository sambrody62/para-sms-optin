Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Para ("we," "us"), a personal task assistant operated by Samuel Brody as a sole proprietor, handles information in connection with its SMS notification service sent from (856) 460-5603.

Information we collect

When you opt in to text notifications, we collect the mobile phone number you provide (or text from), the date and method of your consent (texting START or submitting the web form), and your opt-in and opt-out history. While the service operates, we also process the content of messages sent to and from your number (task requests, reminders, confirmations) in order to deliver the service.

How we use information

Your phone number and message content are used solely to deliver the notifications you requested: task reminders, daily digests, calendar event reminders, task confirmations, and replies to requests you send. We do not use your information for advertising or marketing.

Sharing of SMS opt-in data and mobile information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, or sold to, any third party under any circumstances. Information is shared only with the messaging carrier and our messaging delivery provider (Twilio) to the extent technically necessary to transmit the messages you requested.

Data retention and deletion

Opt-in records are kept while your subscription is active. Message history is retained only as long as needed to operate the assistant. You may request deletion of your phone number and associated data at any time by emailing sambrody34@gmail.com.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel at any time. Reply HELP or email us for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
