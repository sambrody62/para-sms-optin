Privacy Policy · Effective August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Para ("we," "us"), a personal task assistant operated by Sam Brody, handles information in connection with its SMS notification service.

Information we collect

When you sign up for text notifications, we collect the mobile phone number you provide and your consent record. While the service operates, we also process the content of messages sent to and from your number (task requests, reminders, confirmations) in order to deliver the service.

How we use information

Your phone number and message content are used solely to deliver the notifications you requested: task reminders, daily digests, calendar event reminders, and task confirmations. We do not use your information for advertising or marketing.

Sharing — SMS consent and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, or sold to, any third party under any circumstances. Information is shared only with the messaging carrier and delivery provider (Twilio) to the extent technically necessary to transmit the messages you requested.

Data retention and deletion

Message history is retained only as long as needed to operate the assistant. You may request deletion of your phone number and associated data at any time by emailing sambrody34@gmail.com.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP or email us for assistance.
